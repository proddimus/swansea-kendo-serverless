Training Times

We run two sessions each week. Please check the Swansea Kendo Club Facebook page to confirm current times before attending.

New to Kendo? Visit our Getting Started page to find out about our beginner course.

Swansea Bay Sports Park

Our Sunday session is open to beginners (via our beginner course) and anyone that has completed the beginner course.

  • Sunday 9:00 am – 11:00 am, Multi-Use Room
  • Swansea Bay Sports Park, Sketty Ln, Sketty, Swansea, SA2 8QB
  • what3words: fortunate.limes.legend

Thursday Practice

Thursday practice is open to Kendoka that have their own armour. These sessions focus on progressing the fundamentals learned at Sunday sessions and usually end with Jigeiko.

  • Thursday 7:15 pm – 8:30 pm
  • Location available on request
